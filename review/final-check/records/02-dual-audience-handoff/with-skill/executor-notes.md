## 2 Instructions that were unclear, contradictory, or that I did not follow

1. **Sibling threshold vs "from this same document".** SKILL.md Step 3 and rubric A3 say AI-input plus archive over about 60 lines, or one block over about 25, goes to a sibling file. My AI-input block A is about 125 lines. I kept it inside `report.md` because the user said the agent will continue "from this same document", which I read as the "wants one self-sufficient file" exception. Only the archive went to a sibling. The skill does not say whether a block an agent must act from is covered by that exception; one sentence would settle it.
2. **The glance-panel slot list has no place for the handoff audience's questions.** The audience table says a handoff must let the reader answer "who carries on, what may they do alone, what must they not touch", but "Contents, in this order" (verdict, decisions, risks, key facts, picture, read next) has no slot for it. I dropped "Key facts" to stay under 300 words and used its place for one "Carrying on" line that links to section 4. That deviates from the stated order.
3. **300-word panel budget vs content.** Verdict, six decisions, five risks, a cost line, a picture caption and a stop line did not fit at first (308 words). The checker counts blockquote bullets, the caption, the stop line and link text inside prose, but not tables or link-led Read-next bullets, which invites moving prose into tables; I did not do that, I cut Key facts and shortened lines instead. SKILL.md says "near 300", the checker enforces 300 as a hard Major.
4. **B6 vs B3 (headings).** B3 and SKILL.md praise question headings such as "Q1 Allow XLS above 1M rows? Recommend: split", but B6 wants link-target headings without punctuation. I linked to the Q headings from the risk table, so I stripped punctuation ("Q1 Allow XLS above 1M rows and Alex prefers the ZIP option"). The result is less natural and the skill does not say which rule wins.
5. **"Who decides what by when - recommend" vs sources that name none.** Only Q1 names a person (Dana was asked); Q2, Q4, Q5, Q6 have no named decider, no date and no recommendation. I wrote "budget holder (not named)", "someone to be named", "No recommendation in the notes", and "Dana (suggested)" for Q6. The skeleton's word "recommend:" is stronger than Alex's "I prefer (a)", so I wrote "Alex prefers (a)" (rewriting.md warns about stronger attribution). Unclear whether "Dana (suggested)" is acceptable as the "who".
6. **No rule for "today".** The skill requires a date under the title and tells me to recompute weekdays, but the sources conflict: the work log says leave starts 2026-10-17, the user said "from tomorrow", the system clock said 2026-10-19. Return date (Sat 2026-11-07) agrees in all of them. I used the system date, flagged the mismatch in block A and in my reply, and did not guess further.
7. **"(suggested)" is required but its definition has no home.** I added a second italic line under the title explaining "(suggested)" and "inferred"; C1 and the skeleton assume one metadata line. The checker still passes.
8. **rewriting.md not applied.** The inputs are three short notes (82 lines in total), not "a large existing document nobody can read", and `source_set.py` handles one source with verbatim slices. Rubric G1 ("nothing is lost") still seemed right for notes-to-handoff, so I kept byte-exact copies with checksums and wrote a note-to-report map by hand. The skill does not say what to do for several small inputs.
9. **Legend rule vs diagrams.md.** SKILL.md wants a legend where colour carries meaning; diagrams.md warns that legend swatches distort layouts. I put the status word in every node and described arrows, ovals and the dashed edge in the caption instead of drawing a legend.
10. **Node count.** D2 wants every named participant as its own node; the diagram guidance says about a dozen nodes. I drew all 16 (11 tasks, 5 answers) at 2245 px and did not split into overview plus zoom; labels are about 9-11 px at 800 px wide. Whether that is "legible" is a judgment call.
11. **Duplication test vs self-sufficient AI-input (A4).** Section 4 ("must not do") and block A ("Limits") overlap, and the panel's decision lines overlap section 3. I kept both on purpose: humans need the boundaries in the human zone, the agent needs them self-contained. The skill does not say how much overlap is acceptable here.
12. **Chat reply path.** The reply must give "folder plus file name", but documents must not contain machine-local paths. I gave a path starting at the eval folder name. The skill does not say what to do when there is no repo root.
13. **Separating the source's rules from mine in the AI-input block.** The skill says to mark proposals "(suggested)" but not how to keep a list of hard limits readable; I split Limits into "From Alex's notes" (1-6) and "Proposed by the writer (suggested)" (7-8).
