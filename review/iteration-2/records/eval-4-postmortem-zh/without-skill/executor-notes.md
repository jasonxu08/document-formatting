# Notes on report.md (10-09 order/payment incident postmortem)

## 1. Structure choices and why

- **Decision first.** The reader who must act is 张伟, who has to choose among six improvement items, while the whole team needs the gist. So §1 gives what happened, impact, why it took 105 minutes, and a boxed decision ask. The key fact is that the six items total 17 person-days against about 10 available, so they cannot all fit. I recommend one package (A4, A3, A6, A1, A5 = 9 days, A2 deferred) and name the alternative (A2, A3, A4, A6 = 10 days). The real choice is how to spend the last 8 days: A2 alone, or A1 + A5.
- **Timeline in two layers.** §3.1 is a phase breakdown (figure + table: 7 / 11 / 38 / 12 / 37 min, which sums to 105) and shows where the time went and which improvement item touches which phase. §3.2 is the detailed table that merges timeline.md and the chat, with a source column. Rows where the two sources disagree carry a † and are explained in Appendix B, so the body stays readable.
- **Cause → evidence → item table (§4.2).** It makes each improvement item traceable to a cause. It also exposes two gaps: the chat says the config center "doesn't distinguish per-environment defaults" (not in the timeline's cause list, no item covers it), and nothing targets the response and recovery phases (48 of 105 minutes).
- **Improvement section (§6).** A compact table (owner, person-days, running total), then one short bullet per item on what it would and would not have changed, a two-option comparison, and a budget figure. Effect statements are marked as estimates. I did not invent owners or estimates for the new candidate A7.
- **Open questions (§7)** are only the ones that change the decision, each with who can answer and why it matters. The full source-reconciliation log is in Appendix B, not the body.
- **Appendices.** A is the service list plus each service's role. B is the reconciliation log. C is the raw chat, verbatim in a fenced block, 28 lines. A script checked it is identical to chat-log.txt after normalising CRLF.
- **Figures.** Three PNGs (phases, propagation/dependency graph from services.csv, budget vs 10 days) generated by `figures/make_figures.py`. I chose images over Mermaid so they render in any viewer. They have alt text and captions, and the report uses relative paths, so `figures/` must travel with `report.md`.
- **Language and typography.** The report is in Chinese. I used full-width punctuation, a space between CJK and Latin/digits, and “ ” quotes. Bold spans avoid boundaries that CommonMark mis-parses next to CJK punctuation. I checked with markdown-it: 10 tables, no stray `**`, anchors resolve.
- **Tone.** I added a one-line "no blame, process and systems only" statement and kept wording neutral. Remove it if that is not your team's norm.

## 2. What I'm unsure about

- **Is "about 10 person-days" a team total or per person?** I assumed a team total that can be allocated by owner, and said so in §6.1. Option 1 only works in parallel if 周杰 and 赵敏 each actually have 3–4 days. I also did not re-estimate the 17 days, which come from the timeline.
- **How I reconciled timeline.md and the chat.** The chat wins for who did what and when. The timeline wins for monitoring data (push time, the 14:05 P99). The disagreements, listed in Appendix B:
  - The timeline says the DB detour cost "~25 min", but the chat shows 8 minutes (14:27–14:35). 25 minutes is 14:27–14:52, the whole no-lead stretch. I reported both.
  - The timeline says payment CPU was above 90% for ~40 min, but the chat implies about 69 min (14:22 to 15:31). I left the duration out of the body.
  - The timeline credits 赵敏 for spotting the full thread pool at 14:58, but 王磊's thread dump at 14:52 found it. I credited both.
  - The first response was 14:12, not 14:20.
  - The alert text says "P99 > 5s, sustained 5 min", which implies a 5-minute window on top of the 5s threshold. That does not square with "P99 > 3s only at 14:05". So A3 (threshold only) may save just 1–2 minutes. This is my inference, and §7 asks 王磊 to confirm the rule.
- **Payment → order retry direction.** services.csv says order → payment and payment → notification only, yet the timeline and chat describe payment retrying order. I drew it as a dashed edge flagged "not in services.csv" and listed it as an open question.
- **Did the push also change payment's and the gateway's own pools?** The push reached three services, but only the order service's change is documented. This affects how much of the payment CPU spike was retries versus its own pool limit.
- **Was CFG-2231 reviewed?** Unknown. This is the main uncertainty on A6's value. A1's value likewise depends on the min/max actually chosen. Whether 20 is a legitimate test value is unknown, which is why I proposed A7.
- **SLO arithmetic.** 99.97% → 99.93% is 0.04 pp, or 80% of a 0.05% monthly budget. I derived this myself. The availability basis (request vs time based) is not stated; 105 minutes of full outage would give about 99.77%, so I assume it is request-based.
- **Author, date and addressee.** I don't know who is sending this, so there is no author field. The report date is 2026-10-17 (today). I treated 张伟's role as given.
- **Rendering.** I could not view it in a real Markdown viewer. Preview via the browser tool failed (file:// blocked, and screenshots can only be saved inside the repo). Checks were structural (markdown-it) plus viewing each PNG.
